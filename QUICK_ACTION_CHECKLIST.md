## 🚨 Red Flags (Check Immediately If...)

⚠️ **No visitors after 2 weeks**
- Is website live?
- Is Search Console set up?
- Is sitemap submitted?

⚠️ **No impressions in Search Console**
- Are pages indexed?
- Check for crawl errors
- Verify robots.txt is correct

⚠️ **Traffic suddenly drops**
- Check if site is down
- Look for Google penalties
- Review recent changes

⚠️ **High bounce rate (>80%)**
- Is content relevant?
- Is site loading fast?
- Are there broken links?

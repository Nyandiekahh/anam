# Quick Start: Getting ANAM CBO on Google

## Step-by-Step Guide to Submit Your Website to Google

### Step 1: Google Search Console (CRITICAL - Do This First!)

1. **Go to**: https://search.google.com/search-console/
2. **Sign in** with your Google account (use anamcbo@gmail.com if you have one)
3. **Add Property** (your website)
   - Choose: "URL prefix"
   - Enter: https://anamcbo.org (or your actual domain)
   - Click "Continue"

4. **Verify Ownership** - Choose one method:
   - **HTML file upload** (easiest):
     - Download the verification file
     - Upload it to /home/x/anam/public/ folder
     - Rebuild and deploy your site
     - Click "Verify" in Google Search Console
   
   - **HTML tag** (alternative):
     - Copy the meta tag provided
     - Add it to /home/x/anam/public/index.html in the <head> section
     - Rebuild and deploy
     - Click "Verify"

5. **Submit Sitemap**:
   - In Search Console, go to "Sitemaps" (left sidebar)
   - Enter: `sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"
   - Google will start crawling your pages!
